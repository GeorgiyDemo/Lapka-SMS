Lapka SMS is a privacy-focused SMS messaging app with built-in encryption and steganography.

Features:
- End-to-end encryption for SMS messages using AES-GCM
- Steganography: encrypted messages look like normal text (Russian or English words)
- Multiple encoding schemes: Base64, Cyrillic Base64, Russian Words, English Words
- QR code key exchange for easy setup
- Emoji fingerprints to verify shared keys
- Auto-delete encrypted messages after a configurable timeout
- Per-conversation encryption keys
- Material Design UI with themes and dark mode
- Full-featured SMS client: group messaging, MMS, search, archiving

Based on QKSMS, an open-source SMS app. Lapka SMS adds strong encryption so your private conversations stay private, even over insecure SMS channels.
